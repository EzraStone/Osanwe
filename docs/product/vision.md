# Product vision

## Mission

Humans deserve to preserve their dignity when using our greatest invention.

Osanwë exists so a person can ask a machine a private question without first becoming an account,
a profile, or an advertising subject. Privacy is not a premium cosmetic feature. Access is not
reserved for people with a credit card, a permanent address, or the freedom to identify themselves.

## The product promise

Osanwë is privacy-preserving access infrastructure for AI models:

- no conventional user account;
- no server-side conversation history;
- no prompt-based advertising or behavioral profile;
- payment and inference separated wherever the payment method permits it;
- one clear place to see what each participant can learn; and
- provider choice without pretending every provider has the same privacy policy.

The north star is simple: payment should prove entitlement to a bounded service, not become a label
attached to a person's thoughts.

## Who it is for

The first product should work for students, people with limited income, developers,
privacy-conscious users, and people living under political restriction. Those audiences have
different budgets and threat models, but they share a need for honest controls that do not require
specialized cryptographic knowledge.

## Company posture

Osanwë should become sustainable enough to operate reliably and fund careful security work.
Break-even is an acceptable early outcome. Growth, fundraising, and feature breadth do not outrank
the dignity promise.

## Design character

The experience should be calm, legible, and restrained: warm brown and cream, minimal decoration,
and language that treats the user as an adult. Its Catholic inspiration appears in its view of
human dignity and moral limits, not as a barrier to people of another faith or none.

Every design choice reflects a vision of humanity.
